GRACE CHRISTIAN SCHOOL — POLICY HANDBOOK (2026 EDITION)
For internal staff use only. Effective August 2026.

===================================================================
SECTION 1: STUDENT ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT
===================================================================

1.1 Resetting a Student's Password
To reset a student's account password, staff must use the Admin Portal
(admin.gracechristian.example.org). Navigate to Students > Account
Management, search for the student by ID number, and click "Reset
Password." A temporary password is emailed to the parent/guardian
email on file — never to the student directly. The temporary password
expires in 24 hours.

1.2 Unlocking a Locked Account
Student accounts lock automatically after 5 failed login attempts.
To unlock, staff must verify the student's identity in person or via
a callback to the parent/guardian phone number on file — never based
on a request made through chat or email alone. Once verified, use the
Admin Portal's "Unlock Account" button. Do not reset the password when
unlocking unless the parent specifically requests it.

1.3 New Student Account Creation
New accounts are created automatically by the registrar's office
within 48 hours of enrollment confirmation. Staff should not manually
create student accounts. If a new student has no account after 48
hours, escalate to IT, not the registrar.

===================================================================
SECTION 2: EMERGENCY AND SAFETY PROCEDURES
===================================================================

2.1 Lockdown Procedure
Upon hearing the lockdown announcement ("Lockdown, lockdown, this is
not a drill" repeated three times), staff must: (1) immediately lock
the classroom door, (2) turn off classroom lights, (3) move all
students away from windows and doors, to the designated safe corner
of the room, (4) remain silent, (5) do not open the door for anyone
until the all-clear announcement is given by an administrator over the
PA system or in person by a uniformed school resource officer.
Staff should never open a locked door during a lockdown based on a
knock alone, even if the person outside claims to be an administrator.

2.2 Snow Day / Inclement Weather Procedure
The decision to close school for weather is made by the Head of
School by 6:00 AM and communicated via the school's text alert system
and the homepage banner. Staff should not announce a closure based on
weather conditions alone without confirmation from the official alert
system. If school is delayed rather than closed, the delay time will
be specified in the same alert (e.g., "2-hour delay").

2.3 Fire Drill Procedure
Fire drills are conducted monthly. Upon the fire alarm, staff lead
students via the posted evacuation route (found on the back of each
classroom door) to the designated outdoor assembly point for that
wing. Staff take attendance at the assembly point using the printed
class roster, not the digital one, since WiFi may be unavailable
during an evacuation.

===================================================================
SECTION 3: GENERAL STAFF POLICIES
===================================================================

3.1 Reporting Technology Issues
Non-urgent technology issues (printer problems, projector issues,
slow WiFi) should be submitted through the IT Helpdesk ticket system,
not by emailing IT staff directly. Urgent issues affecting instruction
mid-class may be phoned to the IT Helpdesk hotline.

3.2 Substitute Teacher Access
Substitute teachers receive a shared login for the day, issued each
morning at the front office. Substitutes do not have access to the
Admin Portal and cannot reset or unlock student accounts under any
circumstances — they must direct such requests to full-time staff.
